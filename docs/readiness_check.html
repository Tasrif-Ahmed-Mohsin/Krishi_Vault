What we can claim, and what we can show

Before the presentation we checked every promise in the KrishiVault plan against the data and software we really have. This page explains the plan and the prototype in plain words, marks each claim, and gives the safe way to say it to the judges.

Checked on 13 October 2026, against the KrishiVault code, the Mati Kohon data, and public sources.

The idea in one minute

Many farmers in Bangladesh only have a button phone and cannot use apps. KrishiVault is a free phone service for them. The farmer listens and answers by pressing keys or speaking. KrishiVault warns him before weather or disease costs him money, tells him in taka what to buy, when to sell and when to store, and connects him to a cold storage, a buyer, a fertilizer group or a lender. Potato in Bogura is our example crop.

The plan

The complete product we will build after the competition.

  • Danger alerts by phone call (disease, rain, heat, cold, flood, price crash)
  • Sell, store or group-sell advice in taka, with a cold-storage booking or group sale in the same call
  • Fertilizer: how much, the fair price, and buying together
  • Loans from a partner instead of dadon, using the farmer's own records
  • The farmer's season record, কৃষি খাতা, filled by others, not typed by him
  • Talking to the AI on a real phone call through our own phone line
  • Web screens for officers, cold storages, buyers, dealers, lenders and DAE

The prototype

What we can really show on the day.

  • Real SMS to a judge's phone (works today)
  • Real voice calls to a judge's phone (once Awaj turns on broadcast)
  • Talking to the AI inside an app: it hears Bangla, writes it down, and answers in a Bangla voice
  • Today's real NASA alerts for any upazila
  • The sell or store decision in taka
  • Simple example screens for the officer, cold storage, buyer and lender

Words you will hear

NASA POWER
Free daily NASA weather (temperature, rain, humidity) for any place.
OPERA
A NASA satellite product that maps surface water, which we use for flood water.
HLS
NASA's Landsat and Sentinel-2 images at 30 metres, which we use to see where crops are green.
SRDI
Soil Resource Development Institute. Its union fertilizer cards say how much fertilizer each crop needs.
DAM
Department of Agricultural Marketing. Publishes daily market prices.
BBS
Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics. Publishes crop area and production by district.
DAE / SAAO
Department of Agricultural Extension, and its field officer in each union.
Dadon
An advance loan from a trader that forces the farmer to sell to him, often cheaply.
Awaj / REVE
Bangladeshi services we use to make voice calls (Awaj) and send SMS (REVE).
096 number, Asterisk
A licensed internet phone number and the call server software for our future phone line.
Speech-to-text, text-to-speech
Turning the farmer's voice into words, and turning our answer into a spoken Bangla voice.
Gemini
Google's AI. We use it to understand what the farmer said and to word the answer; it is never allowed to change a number.
