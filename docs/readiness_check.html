Updated on 14 October 2026 for the storage-and-selling plan. Checked against the KrishiVault code and its data (BBS production, WFP prices, team field notes).

The idea in one minute

A potato farmer in Bogura pays a cold storage about ৳250 for an 84 kg sack, for up to nine months. The rent is the same for one month or nine. Afraid that prices will fall, many farmers sell within one to three months, before prices rise; in a big-crop year prices may never rise at all. Traders, with the cash and the information, store and sell later. KrishiVault answers the farmer's question, "store or sell, and until when?", in taka and with honest odds, and turns the answer into an action: a cold-storage booking, a group order, a loan against the stored sacks instead of a forced sale, or a better buyer. Farmers use any phone; partners use a web dashboard. The farmer pays nothing.

1. PredictionThe season's production and the price 1 to 9 months ahead, always with a range.
2. Store or sellSell now, store until the best month, or sell part: after rent, losses, transport and interest.
3. Market accessBuyers ranked by the money left in the farmer's hand.
4. Bank loansStored sacks, confirmed by the cold storage, back a loan; the bank decides.
5. ExportWhere to buy, and which markets pay after every cost.
6. Group storageNeighbours combine sacks for shared trucks and, if agreed, a lower rent.
7. ProfitEvery cost and sale: profit per kg, sack, maund and harvest.

The engine and the data behind it are real: 13 seasons of BBS production for every district, two and a half years of WFP market prices, and a tested storage engine. Backtested on the 2024 and 2025 seasons with a model that had not seen them, it called store-or-sell right in both (two seasons only: a first check, not proof). The gaps are a longer price history, field-checked costs, and real partners (cold storage, bank, buyer, exporter). We present those as the plan, not as finished work.

Prediction

Potato production forecast for Bangladesh and every district, with a rangeReady
We haveBBS production for 64 districts, 2012-13 to 2024-25, and a trend forecast with a range from past errors. It shows the glut years: 2016-17 and 2024-25 were 6-7% above the trend.
Say"We forecast the crop from 13 seasons of official statistics, with a range." Weather is not in it yet.
Price outlook 1 to 9 months after the harvestSmall work
We haveA statistical outlook: the usual seasonal shape, made steeper or flatter by the crop size, corrected by the season's own prices, with 2,000 simulated paths. Fitted on only two full seasons of WFP prices. Backtest (Forecasts page): on the two seasons, each predicted without seeing it, it missed by 11% on average against 52% for the usual seasonal shape, and called store-or-sell right in both. Only 12 months of prices, and the crop-size link had to be assumed for the test. The dashboard's season replays are fair too: only what was known on that date.
Say"A statistical outlook with ranges, built on official production and market data." Not "our AI predicts the price." "Tested on two past seasons" is fair; "proven" is not. Load the DAM history and re-run the backtest before saying more.

Before the presentation

In order of importance. Each one turns a weak claim into one we can show.

  1. Send one live SMS to a team phone from a laptop (with the keys set and the team's go-ahead); the REVE gateway hangs up on cloud machines.
  2. Visit two Bogura cold storages and five farmers with the field check guide (farmer_interview_guide.html): this season's rent and sack size, the advance and its interest, losses, carrying and selling costs, when farmers sell.
  3. Get the DAM or FAO monthly price history and re-run the backtest (built; it re-runs on any longer history).
  4. Try the Google voice in the call preview (Gemini is done), and speech with a Bogura speaker.
  5. Talk to one bank or microfinance lender about loans against stored potatoes.
  6. Get two or three real export quotes.
  7. Rehearse the new pitch deck (9 slides, 2 minutes) against a timer.

Words you will hear

Cold storage (হিমাগার)
A refrigerated store where potatoes keep for up to about nine months. Rent is charged per sack for the season.
Sack (বস্তা)
The cold-storage sack: 84 kg. A maund (মণ) is 37.3 kg, so one sack is about 2.25 maunds.
Crop gap
How far this season's production is above or below its long-term trend. A big positive gap means a glut.
Rise strength
How strongly prices rise after the harvest this season: 1 is the usual rise (almost double by December), 0 is flat.
Range (8 in 10)
Of 2,000 simulated price paths, 8 in 10 fall inside it; 1 in 10 is below (the "bad case").
Break-even price
The price at which storing gives the same money as selling today.
Collateral, LTV
What backs a loan (the stored sacks), and the share of its value a bank will lend.
Landed cost
Everything an exporter pays to get a kilo to the foreign buyer.
BBS, WFP, DAM
Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (production), the World Food Programme (market prices), Department of Agricultural Marketing (daily prices).
Number lock
Gemini may reword the answer, but if any number changes, KrishiVault uses its own wording.
REVE
The Bangladeshi service we use to send SMS.
