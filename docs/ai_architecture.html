How the AI and the voice work

What runs in the prototype today, and how the farmer's Bangla conversation will work after the pilot, following the plan document. The short version for judges: AI listens and speaks Bangla; fixed rules do the money; every number the farmer hears comes from the engine and is checked.

The one rule

The plan calls KrishiVault "AI-powered". In practice that means three different things, and each does only its own job.

Statistics where the future is uncertainThe price outlook: the usual seasonal shape, made steeper or flatter by the crop size (BBS), corrected by real prices (WFP), 2,000 simulated paths. Always a range.
Exact sums where the money isThe storage engine: rent, 8.65% weight loss, interest, net profit after the ৳14 a kg growing cost. Fixed rules; every taka can be explained.
Language AI only to understand and phraseGoogle's speech services and Gemini hear and speak Bangla. They never set a number: the number lock checks every one.

One turn of today's call

The farmer's conversation after the pilot

The plan's channels are Grameenphone's: a Bangla voice line (a VAS short code, call revenue shared 50/50), a USSD code, and SMS. A field agent at the haat or the cold storage can do the same for a farmer. The conversation is guided, not open chat: it asks only for what is missing, reads numbers back, and hands over to a person when it is unsure.

What changes from today

  • Phone audio instead of the browser: the media server (Asterisk) takes the call's audio and keys; streaming Google speech-to-text in Bangla (bn-BD) replaces the browser's, with Whisper tested on the same recordings.
  • Turn-taking: the server detects when the farmer stops speaking and lets them interrupt the answer (barge-in). The keypad still works at every step.
  • The same call steps: menu, questions, read-back, answer, one action, consent (press 8), helper (press 9). This logic exists today in conversation.py.
  • More data in: 15 years of DAM prices, DAE planted area in January, NASA POWER season weather, cold-storage fill from partners. Machine-learning forecasts (the plan names XGBoost, Prophet and LSTM) come after that history is loaded and backtested against today's outlook.
  • Partners act through APIs: receipts from the cold-storage portal, loan applications to a bank or MFI with the farmer's consent, lots to buyers. Payments go bank to farmer or by mobile money.

Questions judges may ask about the AI

Which AI do you use?

Google Gemini to understand what the farmer said when our Bangla parser cannot place a number, and to reword the answer so it sounds natural. Google's speech services to hear and speak Bangla. The forecast is a statistical model with 2,000 simulated price paths. The decisions are exact sums, not AI.

What if the AI gets a number wrong?

It cannot reach the farmer. Every number Gemini returns must appear in what the farmer said; every number is read back for a keypad "yes"; and the number lock rejects any reworded answer whose numbers differ from the engine's, falling back to the template.

Can it understand the Bogura dialect?

Our parser reads northern forms such as "হামার", "টেকা", "নাগবে". The demo sentence is in dialect. Speech recognition with real Bogura speakers is the first test of the pilot; the keypad works whatever happens.

Is it a chatbot?

No. It is a guided call: it asks only what it needs (quantity, price, cash needed), reads it back, answers in taka with the risk, and offers one action. Open chat would be slower and harder to check, and the money must be exact.

Does it work on a button phone without internet?

Yes: a voice call, a USSD menu and SMS all work on any phone. The farmer needs no app and no data. The prototype shows the call and the USSD menu in the browser because there is no phone line yet.

Can you really predict potato prices with AI?

We give a range, never a single price. Tested on two past seasons it had not seen, the outlook missed by 11% on average, against 52% for "prices always rise the usual way", and got store-or-sell right both times. Two seasons is a first test; with 15 years of DAM prices we will test it again before trying machine-learning models.

What does a call cost, and who pays?

The farmer pays no fee to KrishiVault. In the plan, calls to the voice line are charged at a Grameenphone VAS rate (৳2-3 a minute) and the revenue is shared 50/50. Speech and Gemini costs are per call; caching common answers keeps them down. Exact costs come from the pilot.

What about farmers' data?

Phone numbers are masked on every partner screen. A farmer is counted in government figures only after pressing 8, without name or number, and a district with fewer than 5 farmers is hidden. A loan application goes to a bank only when the farmer applies.

What if the farmer is still confused?

After two misunderstandings, or when the farmer presses 9, a KrishiVault helper calls back with everything the call already collected.

Don't say"Farmers can already call our AI."
