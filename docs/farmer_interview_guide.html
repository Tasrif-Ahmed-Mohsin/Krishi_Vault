Farmer Interview Guide

কৃষকের সমস্যা জানার প্রশ্নমালা — আলু চাষি, বগুড়া

Why we are doing this. KrishiVault helps potato farmers with button phones: danger alerts, sell-or-store advice in taka, fair fertilizer prices, and a season record. Before we present it, we want to hear the problems from farmers themselves. Their real answers make our problem slide stronger and check our design.

The golden rule. Only real answers from real farmers go on the slides. Never invent a quote or a number. If a judge asks "how many farmers did you talk to, and where?", we must answer with what we really did.

Who to talk to

  • 10 to 15 potato farmers in Bogura. Mix char land (Sariakandi) and a central upazila.
  • Small and larger farmers, some women farmers, and farmers with and without cold-storage access.
  • About 20 minutes each. Two of us: one asks, one writes.

Before you start: read this aloud

"আমরা ছাত্র, একটা কৃষি প্রকল্পের জন্য আপনার অভিজ্ঞতা জানতে চাই। আপনার নাম কোথাও প্রকাশ হবে না। ছবি বা রেকর্ড শুধু আপনার অনুমতি নিয়ে। আমরা কোনো টাকা বা সাহায্যের প্রতিশ্রুতি দিচ্ছি না। কথা বলবেন?"

We are students and want to learn from your experience for a farming project. Your name will not be published. Photos or recordings only with your permission. We are not promising money or help. Will you talk to us?

How to ask

  • Ask an open question first, then ask for the number.
  • Don't suggest answers. Ask "why did you sell then?", not "did you sell because of dadon?"
  • Use their units: মণ, বিঘা, বস্তা, কেজি.
  • Write numbers, not impressions.
  • Write exact words for anything that could be a quote.
  • Ask before every photo or recording. Mark it on the form.

How this guide is laid out

  • Pages 2 to 4: the questions, in eight groups. ★ marks a hero question whose answer is strongest on a slide.
  • Page 5: the answer form. Print one for each farmer.
  • Page 6: the tally sheet, to add up the numbers after the visits.
  • Page 7: how to turn real answers into slides, and what we expect to hear.

"আপনার সময়ের জন্য ধন্যবাদ। আপনার কথাগুলো আমাদের সেবা ঠিকভাবে বানাতে সাহায্য করবে।"

Turning real answers into slides

Fill these templates only with what you collected. Write the sample size every time ("of 12 farmers").

Voices from Bogura. Two or three short quotes in Bangla with an English line, signed farmer, Sariakandi (no name unless they agree).
The price gap. "Sold at ৳___/kg in [month]; the market reached ৳___/kg by [month]." Use the average across farmers.
Fertilizer. "___ of ___ farmers paid more than the official ৳1,350 for a bag of urea; the average paid was ৳___."
The channel. "___ of ___ use button phones; ___ would trust a Bangla alert call."
Dadon. "___ of ___ took dadon; ___ had to sell to their lender."
Spraying. "Average ___ sprays a season, mostly on the dealer's advice."
Proof the prototype works. "___ of ___ farmers repeated our demo advice correctly."

What we expect to hear: prepare, don't quote

These come from research and news. Use them to know what to ask more about, never as farmer answers.

  • Fertilizer overcharging is common. In August 2026, urea sold in Joypurhat for ৳1,900 to ৳1,950 a bag against the official ৳1,350 (The Daily Observer, observerbd.com/news/589247).
  • Late blight is a constant worry. In Bogura, the weather favours late blight on about 40 days of a typical potato season (25 seasons of NASA data).
  • Many farmers sell early for cash or because of dadon, even when they know prices will rise. Research finds that price information alone rarely raises what farmers are paid (Fafchamps and Minten, India; Aker and Fafchamps, Niger).
If what farmers tell us is different from what we expected, say so on the slides. Judges respect "we went, we listened, and we changed our design because of what farmers told us."
