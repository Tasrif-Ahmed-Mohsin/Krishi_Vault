Field Check Guide: Potato After the Harvest

আলু রাখবেন না বেচবেন: কৃষক আর হিমাগারের কাছে প্রশ্নমালা, বগুড়া

Why we are doing this. KrishiVault tells a potato farmer whether to sell now, store until a month, or sell part and store the rest. The sums use the cold-storage rent, the sack size, weight loss, carrying and selling costs, and loan interest. Some of these come from our field notes and the 2016 value-chain study our plan quotes; many are still our guesses. This check replaces the guesses with real Bogura numbers, tells us when and why farmers sell, and checks the plan's figures (64% sold at harvest, 8.65% storage loss).

The golden rule. Only real answers from real people go into the engine or onto a slide. Never invent a number or a quote. If a judge asks "how many farmers and cold storages did you talk to, and where?", we answer with what we really did.

Who to talk to

  • At least 5 potato farmers (10 is better) in Kahaloo, the plan's pilot upazila (haats such as Dhaper Hat and Bibir Pukur), and in Shibganj or Sariakandi.
  • Mix: farmers who stored in a cold storage last season and farmers who sold at the harvest; small and larger; some women farmers.
  • 2 cold storages: the manager or owner (page 5).
  • About 25 minutes a farmer, 20 a cold storage. Two of us: one asks, one writes.

Good timing: in October the potatoes stored in March are still in the cold storages, so farmers remember the rent, the advance and the weight they put in.

How to ask

  • Ask an open question first, then ask for the number.
  • Don't suggest answers. Ask "why did you sell then?", not "did you sell because of dadon?"
  • Use their units: মণ, বিঘা, বস্তা, কেজি. Write the unit with every number.
  • Write numbers, not impressions. "About ৳250 a sack" beats "cheap".
  • Write exact words for anything that could be a quote.
  • Ask before every photo or recording; mark it on the form.

Before you start: read this aloud

"আমরা ছাত্র, একটা কৃষি প্রকল্পের জন্য আপনার অভিজ্ঞতা জানতে চাই। আপনার নাম কোথাও প্রকাশ হবে না। ছবি বা রেকর্ড শুধু আপনার অনুমতি নিয়ে। আমরা কোনো টাকা, ঋণ বা সাহায্যের প্রতিশ্রুতি দিচ্ছি না। কথা বলবেন?"

We are students and want to learn from your experience for a farming project. Your name will not be published. Photos or recordings only with your permission. We are not promising money, loans or help. Will you talk to us?

To cold storages: say the same, and say clearly that KrishiVault has no partners yet; the cold storages, banks and buyers in our prototype are made up (demo). We are collecting information, not signing anyone.

How to play the call in the field

With a laptop (best): start KrishiVault on it before the visit (docs/SCREENS.md), open /call, choose the season "Next harvest", press New call, then 1, and type the farmer's own numbers on the keypad, each followed by #: maunds, today's price per kg, cash needed (0 if none) and, if it asks, the days; then 1 to confirm. Let the farmer listen, then ask questions 22 and 23. The keypad works without internet; the Bangla voice may need it, so if there is no sound, read the answer from the screen.
Without a laptop: read the example answer below aloud, slowly, after saying: "ধরুন একজন কৃষকের ৮০ মণ আলু উঠেছে, ফড়িয়া কেজিতে ১৬ টাকা দিতে চায়, আর ৪ দিনের মধ্যে ২৫ হাজার টাকা দরকার। আমাদের ফোনটা তাকে এটা বলবে:" (Imagine a farmer with 80 maunds, offered ৳16 a kg, who needs ৳25,000 within 4 days. Our call would tell him this:)
Example answer, word for word from KrishiVault's engine (a forecast for the March 2027 harvest, not a promise)
"পরামর্শ: এখন ১৯ বস্তা বেচুন, হাতে আসবে প্রায় ২৫,০০০ টাকা। বাকি ১৭ বস্তা হিমাগারে রেখে ডিসেম্বর মাসে বেচুন। সব মিলিয়ে গড়ে প্রায় ৫৫,৬০০ টাকা, এখন সব বেচার চেয়ে প্রায় ৮,২০০ টাকা বেশি; দাম খারাপ হলে প্রায় ৩৭,৭০০ টাকা। এতে আপনার ২৫,০০০ টাকার দরকার মিটবে। দাম বাড়বেই এমন কথা নেই, তাই কিছুটা এখনই বেচা নিরাপদ।"

Sell 19 sacks now: about ৳25,000 in hand. Store the other 17 and sell in December. About ৳55,600 in all on average, about ৳8,200 more than selling everything now; about ৳37,700 if prices go badly. That covers your ৳25,000. Prices may not rise, so selling some now is safer.

What to listen for: can the farmer say back how many sacks to sell now and until when to store the rest? Did "দাম খারাপ হলে" (if prices go badly) make sense? Write the farmer's exact words.

What we expect to hear: prepare, don't quote

These are the team's own field notes (14 October 2026). Use them to know what to ask more about, never as answers from this field check.

  • The cold-storage sack is 84 kg; rent is about ৳250 a sack (some ৳300) for up to 9 months, the same for any length of stay. Some cold storages charge by the kg.
  • A cold storage holds 50,000 to 2.5 lakh sacks.
  • Cold storages lend ৳200 to ৳300 a sack; the stored sacks are the security.
  • Potato keeps about 3 months at home.
  • Farmers who store tend to sell within 1 to 3 months, afraid the price will fall; traders store potatoes for sale and release them when prices rise. Farmers mostly store only seed potato.
  • Farmers sell retail (shops and people they know) and at the haat, where traders buy in bulk.
  • The value-chain study our plan quotes (Kahaloo and Munshiganj, 2016 fieldwork): farmers sold 64% at harvest and stored 13.6% for sale; traders bought 97.6% of cold-stored potatoes; cold storage lost 8.65% of the weight. Its taka figures are a decade old.
If an answer surprises you (a rent charged per kg, a different sack size, no flat rent), write it exactly and ask a follow-up. A surprise is the most useful thing this check can find: it changes the engine's sums.
