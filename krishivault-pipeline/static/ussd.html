How to use it

Press Dial / Send to open the menu. Type a number on the keypad (or your keyboard), then Send. For "store or sell" the farmer gives only two numbers, as the plan says: how many sacks, and how much cash is needed now. KrishiVault uses the pre-loaded local price and cost benchmarks.

DEMO Banks, cold storages, buyers and groups are made up. SMS is a dry run. Bangla USSD screens hold fewer characters than English ones; the exact limit must be confirmed with Grameenphone (screens here stay within 160).

What the gateway sends and gets back

Each turn, the USSD gateway sends everything typed so far (for example 1*36*25000); KrishiVault answers CON (keep the session open) or END (close it).
