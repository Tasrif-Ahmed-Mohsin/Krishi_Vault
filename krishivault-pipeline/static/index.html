Why farmers lose money in cold storage কেন কৃষকের ক্ষতি হয়

Rent is flat. A cold storage charges about ৳250 for an 84 kg sack for the season, up to 9 months. One month or nine, the rent is the same.

Farmers sell too early. Afraid that prices will fall further, many sell within 1 to 3 months. They have paid the full rent, but the price has not risen yet. The cold storage empties early and fills again: it gains, the farmer loses.

Big crops make it worse. When the country grows more potato than usual (2016-17, 2024-25), prices stay low all year. Storing then loses money; in a normal or small crop year, prices can nearly double by November.

Traders hold the advantage. Traders buy in bulk at the haat, store, and release potatoes across the country when prices rise. They have the cash and the information; farmers usually store only seed potato.

What KrishiVault does: it forecasts the crop and the price with honest ranges, compares selling now with storing to every month, shows the price at which storing pays, finds better buyers, lets the stored sacks back a bank loan instead of a forced sale, and groups neighbours for cheaper storage and transport.

Store or sell, and until when রাখব না বেচব, কবে বেচব

The storage engine compares selling today with storing for every month until the cold storage empties in December, after rent, weight loss, sacks, carrying, selling costs and loan interest. It recommends one of three options and says how sure it is.

A bank loan against stored potatoes বস্তা জামানতে ব্যাংক ঋণ

Cash needs force early sales. With the sacks in cold storage as security, a bank can lend instead. KrishiVault does not lend: it checks the receipt with the cold storage, values the crop carefully, works out the repayment risk and sends the application. The bank decides. All banks here are DEMO.

Group storage একসাথে হিমাগারে রাখা

Neighbours combine their sacks into one order. Sharing a truck saves money straight away. A cold storage may offer a lower rent for a big order; that saving counts only after the cold storage agrees on its screen, never before.
